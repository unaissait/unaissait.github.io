Privacy Policy for VoxaLoop

Effective date: 8 October 2026
Android package: com.singblend.app

This Privacy Policy explains how VoxaLoop handles information when you use the VoxaLoop Android application. VoxaLoop is an audio, video, karaoke, voice-recording, teleprompter, and media-editing application.

Important: VoxaLoop processes your recorded audio, video, prompts, and imported media primarily on your device. VoxaLoop does not currently operate a cloud service that uploads or stores the contents of your recordings or imported media. Third-party advertising and consent services may process limited device and usage information as described below.

1. Information VoxaLoop accesses

Depending on the features you choose to use, VoxaLoop may request access to:

  • Microphone: used to record your voice for singing, karaoke, voice-over, dubbing, and other recording features.
  • Camera: used when you choose to record video or use camera-based performance features.
  • Photos, videos, audio, or files you select: used when you import reference media into a project.
  • Local app storage: used to save project information, recorded takes, processed audio, previews, and temporary rendering files.

These permissions are used to provide the features you request. VoxaLoop does not use your microphone or camera for advertising purposes.

2. Audio, video, prompts, and imported media

Audio recordings, camera recordings, teleprompter text, project data, and imported media are processed locally on your device for features such as:

  • voice recording and enhancement;
  • karaoke and vocal reduction;
  • reference-video playback;
  • video dubbing and voice-over;
  • teleprompter overlays;
  • mixing, previewing, and exporting media.

VoxaLoop itself does not transmit the content of your recordings, teleprompter text, or imported media to a VoxaLoop-owned server. Exported files are saved locally on your device, such as in your device's media collection, when you choose to export them.

3. Advertising and Google services

VoxaLoop uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to help manage privacy and consent choices where required.

According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app and advertisement interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad/video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crash or performance-related data;
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID or app-set identifiers where available.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related service purposes. Depending on your location and consent choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise limited.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising

4. How information is shared

VoxaLoop does not sell your audio recordings, video recordings, teleprompter text, or imported media.

Limited device and usage information may be processed by Google through the Mobile Ads SDK and consent services as described above. Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

5. Data storage and retention

VoxaLoop project files and recordings are stored locally on your device. They generally remain there until you delete the relevant project, delete the exported file, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

Data processed independently by Google through its advertising services is handled according to Google's own policies and retention practices.

6. Permissions and your choices

You may control camera, microphone, notification, and media permissions through Android system settings. Some VoxaLoop features will not work if the permissions required for those features are disabled.

Android also provides controls related to advertising identifiers and ad privacy. In regions where consent is required, Google's consent interface may provide additional privacy choices.

7. Security

VoxaLoop is designed to keep recording and media-processing operations on the user's device whenever possible. Third-party network communications, including Google advertising services, use the security mechanisms provided by those services. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

8. Children's privacy

VoxaLoop is not specifically designed or directed toward children under 13. If VoxaLoop is later offered to children or included in Google Play's Families program, this Privacy Policy and the app's data practices will be updated as required by applicable law and Google Play policies.

9. No user account

VoxaLoop currently does not require users to create a VoxaLoop account. Because there is no VoxaLoop account system, there is no separate cloud account profile maintained by VoxaLoop to delete. Users can remove locally stored projects and recordings from the app/device.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when VoxaLoop's features, third-party SDKs, data practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
